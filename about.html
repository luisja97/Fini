About

Fino means refined.

Fino Home Detail is an owner-operated Peninsula shop built to clean what a standard visit misses. We do not pretend to be a ten-year award wall. We do publish a checklist, bring a steamer, and keep kitchen cloths off bathroom glass.

Standard

Eco / low-VOC products. HEPA vacuum. Color-coded rags. Written in-scope and out-of-scope.

Steam as default

On every deep clean: floors that allow it, showers, grout, kitchen film, tracks.

How we grow

Deep clean first. Recurring after trust. Same-day review ask. No fake stars.

FAQ

What is the difference vs regular cleaning?

A regular clean maintains a reset house. A Fino deep clean is the reset: baseboards, tracks, grout, steam, and a list you can hold us to.

Is steam safe for my floors or sofa?

Tile and most sealed stone: yes. Raw wood, waxed floors, and unsealed stone: only with approval. Fabric only if the material allows a light pass.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. Steamer, HEPA vac, products, rags. You do not provide chemicals.

Are products pet- and kid-aware?

We use eco / low-VOC products. Still crate or separate pets that chase equipment.

Do I need to be home?

No, if we have access and a lockbox or keypad plan.

Is the oven or fridge interior included?

Exteriors yes. Interiors are add-ons.
