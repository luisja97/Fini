Services

The written checklist.

This is the product. If it is not on this page, it is not in the deep-clean price.

Whole home · included

  • Cobwebs high and low, corners, ceilings
  • Dust blinds, fixtures, reachable fans, décor, frames
  • Wipe sills, rails, switches, door hardware
  • Wipe baseboards and trim
  • Mirrors and interior glass / sliders
  • HEPA vacuum carpets, edges, under furniture we can move
  • Vacuum + mop hard floors (steam on tile/stone)
  • Make beds; change linens only if clean sheets are left out
  • Fluff pillows; straighten living areas (no closet organizing)
  • Empty room trash to the bin

Kitchen · included

  • Dust + wipe cabinet fronts
  • Fridge, oven, dishwasher exteriors
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Stovetop, grates/knobs, hood exterior
  • Small-appliance exteriors
  • Counters, sink, faucet, backsplash
  • Reachable interior kitchen glass
  • Vacuum + mop; trash and recycle

Baths & laundry · included

  • Dust, vents, cabinet fronts
  • Toilet inside, outside, base, behind
  • Sink, faucet, counters, mirrors
  • Shower/tub: soap scum, glass, doors, tracks
  • Washer and dryer exteriors
  • Vacuum + mop; trash

Steam detailing · included on every deep

  • Tile and stone floors after vacuum
  • Shower walls, grout, corners, glass after dwell
  • Tub surround and hard-water edges
  • Kitchen grout / backsplash film
  • Stovetop seams, oven-door glass edges, burner bases
  • Sink rims, faucet bases, drain rings
  • Toilet base and floor line
  • High-touch hardware on safe surfaces
  • Light fabric steam if the material allows
  • Slider / track channels

Method: dwell → agitate → steam into grout and seams → wipe dry. We do not steam raw wood, waxed floors, or unsealed stone without approval.

Add-ons · quoted separately

  • Oven interior · fridge/freezer interior
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Dishwasher interior + filter
  • Behind stove and fridge
  • Interior windows, frames, tracks, screens
  • Wall wash per room · doors both sides
  • Ceiling fans · HVAC vents · fixtures
  • Blind slats detailed
  • Full upholstery / mattress steam
  • Shower restoration (heavy scale or mold in grout)
  • Move-in / move-out empty-home reset

Not included unless quoted

  • Hoarding, biohazard, construction dust
  • Exterior windows above first story
  • Attic, crawlspace, roof
  • Laundry wash/fold, dishes, organizing
  • Pet waste, litter, heavy flea work
  • Heights beyond a safe 6-ft ladder

Quotes are by home size, condition, and parking — not a cheap hourly maid rate. Heavy shower restoration can be its own visit. Recurring is lighter after the first detailing.
